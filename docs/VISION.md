# The Game

Build the spaceship you've always wanted.

Design your engines, weapons, armor, power systems, interiors, and almost anything else you can imagine. Then take your creation into a massive, to-scale solar system to explore, mine, build, and battle other players in their own spaceships.

## Core Rules

### 1. Make It Yours

Total freedom over how your ship looks, works, and feels. Customize its shape, layout, modularity, graphics, cockpit, displays, controls, interiors, HUD, sounds, and more. Import your own art, models, files, code, designs, or whatever else you create.

Your customizations do not change the underlying game rules or physics. Everyone operates within the same fundamental parameters, such as velocity, mass, energy, weapon output, and sensor capabilities, but those capabilities can be expanded through energy, resources, and advanced technology.

How you access, display, and use that information is up to you. Use radar, sensors, navigation, weapons, and other unlocked data streams to create your own interfaces, targeting systems, automation, warning systems, controls, and tools.

Manually fly and fire, build sophisticated automation, or create something nobody else has thought of.

### 2. Same Rules

Multiplayer operates under a shared set of fundamental rules that apply equally to everyone. The default rules define the world, physics, resources, and how technology scales:

- To-scale solar system

- Physics-based movement using Newtonian mechanics and orbital dynamics for normal speeds and distances, with special relativity and time dilation becoming increasingly important as objects approach the speed of light. The game uses practical approximations where needed for performance and gameplay.

- Fixed solar output. The Sun produces a constant amount of energy, and the amount that can be harnessed increases as you move closer to it.

- 300× server speed compared with real time, allowing energy collection, resource processing, and travel between objects to occur much faster.

- Resource extraction. Mine elements and other resources from planets, asteroids, and other celestial bodies.

- Defined scaling for engines, weapons, armor, power systems, energy storage, and other components, with greater flexibility as technology advances.

- These rules are fixed and identical for everyone (the full rule set is available in the Rules file).

## 3. Almost Unlimited Creation

The game should go far beyond traditional crafting systems. Instead of simply unlocking predefined recipes and build trees, players should have the freedom to create their own. Still trying to decide which one here:

Path 1 — Player-Created Systems
Give players the tools to design their own components, recipes, technologies, manufacturing processes, and crafting systems.

Path 2 — Massive Modular Library
Provide an enormous library of components and creations with detailed material and elemental compositions. Players can combine, modify, and experiment with them, creating virtually endless possibilities.

## Technology

Start in a Solar Punk era and progress through:

Solar Punk → Ion → Nuclear → Antimatter → Beyond

Technology should be discovered through experimentation, research, resource gathering, and invention, rather than simply following a fixed progression.

## Philosophy

### 1. Free

100% free to play. Real money cannot buy ships, weapons, resources, upgrades, or competitive advantages.

### 2. Open Source

The long-term goal is a fully open-source game under GNU GPLv2, allowing players to inspect, modify, improve, and host their own versions.

The goal is not simply to give players a large selection of things to build, but to eventually give them the tools to invent what comes next.
