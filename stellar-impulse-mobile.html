🧭 Navigation computer

Why is the Sun hard to reach? Earth carries you around the Sun at about 30 km/s. To move inward you have to cancel that speed, which makes it the most expensive trip in the solar system. The autopilot flies it the efficient way: first it spirals outward until you escape Earth, then it brakes against your motion around the Sun so your orbit shrinks step by step. It uses your real engine, propellant and energy. Speed up time (⏩) while it works.

THRUST MODES

3rd person, orbit modeThrust 1 speeds you up along your path (orbit grows), thrust 2 slows you down, 3/4 push toward/away from the planet, 5/6 tilt the orbit.
1st personThrust keys follow where you look: forward, back, left, right, up, down.

ENGINES & FUEL

🔥 Chemical rocketFuel: liquid hydrogen + liquid oxygen. Energy comes from the fuel's chemistry. Exhaust 4.4 km/s. Strong thrust, uses fuel fast.
⚛ Ion thrusterFuel: xenon gas. Energy comes from your battery/solar panels. Exhaust 29 km/s. Very efficient, very gentle.
☀ Fusion driveFuel: deuterium + helium-3. Energy comes from nuclear fusion of the fuel. Exhaust 0.05c.
✴ Antimatter photon rocketFuel: matter + antimatter. Annihilates completely into light; exhaust = light speed. The only way to 0.99c.
Why exhaust speed mattersA rocket can only go a few times faster than its exhaust before the fuel needed explodes exponentially (the rocket equation). That is why chemical rockets cannot reach even 1,000 km/s.
MassYour mass includes all fuel tanks, and even your stored energy (E = mc²), which matters at relativistic energies.

🚀 STELLAR IMPULSE: how to play

📖 New here, or want to know everything? Read the Stellar Impulse Wiki (also wiki.html in the download).

🚀 Ready to fly: your chemical rocket is selected and carries enough fuel to escape Earth (about 3.9 km/s of burn). Hold W to speed up along your orbit; your orbit stretches out until Earth lets you go. ⚔ Want action right away? Press 0 to call in a wave of raider drones. Fire with the number keys: 1 mining laser · 2 pulse laser · 3 particle beam · 4 railgun · 5 missiles (hold for beams). O switches target, V cycles cameras (chase, cockpit, orbit map). Lasers spread out with distance, so they hit hardest inside 50 km; enemy missiles can be shot down.

Goal: collect solar energy, turn it into more panels and fuel, and build up enough power to fly at relativistic speed (up to 0.99c) while your ship clock falls behind the rest of the universe.
  1. Find your ship. You start in the 3rd-person chase camera, right behind your ship. Press V to cycle cameras: chase → 1st-person cockpit → orbit map. On the orbit map, the orange dot orbiting Earth is you. The green arrow shows where you are moving, the orange loop shows your whole orbit. Drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom, F to look at other planets.
  2. Collect energy. Your solar panels fill your battery while you are in sunlight (ENERGY AVAILABLE, top of screen).
  3. Spend it. Click the big 🛠 ENGINEERING button (or press B). Buy more solar panels and you collect energy faster. Each purchase shows how much faster. Panels work far better close to the Sun.
  4. Fly. You start in orbit mode: W speeds you up along your path, S slows you down, A/D push toward/away from the planet. Press M for fixed axes instead: your ship shows three colored axes: +X, +Y and +Z. Hold D/A to push along +X/−X, W/S along +Y/−Y, E/Q along +Z/−Z (up/down). The axes are fixed to the solar system, so rotate the camera to line them up the way you like. Press M to switch to orbit mode (W = speed up along your path, S = slow down), which is the efficient way to raise or lower an orbit. A red arrow shows your push, and the message at the bottom tells you what is happening.
  5. Views. V: 1st-person cockpit. G: 🚀 ship view, a close-up of your ship (customize it with K). T: 🧭 stabilizer, keeps your view steady. The green 📖 GUIDE button explains every key and lets you change them.
  6. Control time. ⏩ Faster (or the . key) makes everything happen quicker, Space pauses.
  7. Keep your ship alive. Your ship is a machine: every part has mass, uses or makes power, and makes heat that radiators must get rid of. The crew needs oxygen, water and food, and must be shielded from radiation (take shelter with Z during solar storms). Parts wear out and fail: fix them with spare-parts kits. 🛠 ENGINEERING (B) explains what limits your ship and why.
  8. Research and build. Research technologies in 🛠 → 🔬 Research (labs make research points; discoveries give bonus points), then manufacture parts in 🏭 Fabricate. Manufacturing runs on your power over time.
  9. Fight. Press 0 to call in raider drones, then fire with 1–5 or the weapon bar at the bottom. Each wave is bigger; from wave 2 they fire missiles you can shoot down. Beat a wave for missiles and metals. Lose, and you lose your ship. Get more than 400 km away and they give up.
  10. Mine & build. Fly to an asteroid (🧭 NAVIGATE → Asteroids), select the mining laser and hold the 🔥 FIRE key (L) to mine it from within 10 km. Materials unlock new engines and weapons in 🛠 → 🔬 Build. Near Earth you can order fuel from Earth; in deep space you make it from asteroid water.
  11. Engines. Four real drive types, each with its own fuel: 🔥 chemical rocket (rocket fuel), ⚛ ion thruster (xenon + battery power), ☀ fusion drive (deuterium + helium-3) and ✴ antimatter photon rocket. 🛠 ENGINEERING shows what each one does, the top speed your fuel gives you, and how much fuel you would need for a target speed.
  12. Try relativity. In 🛠 ENGINEERING: TESTING “Free +1e21 J”, pick the ✴ Antimatter photon rocket, press “Buy enough to reach 0.99c”, then hold your forward key with time sped up.
