# Asteroids and Mining

Asteroids hold the materials for everything you build ([[Parts]]): water ice, carbon, silicates, iron, nickel and
platinum metals.

## Where they are

- **8 near-Earth asteroids** orbit close to 1 AU, near Earth's path: Kestrel, Halcyon, Tamsin, Ophir, Velka, Corvo,
  Nimbus and Petra. Small (tens to hundreds of metres) and easy to reach.
- **30 main-belt asteroids** between Mars and Jupiter, 2.2 to 3.2 AU from the Sun, up to about 5 km across.

Use 🧭 NAVIGATE → Asteroids: the autopilot escapes your planet, matches the asteroid's orbit and parks 1.5 km away.

## Real objects

Alongside them are real ones, on their real orbits (positions along the orbit are illustrative):
- **Near Earth:** Apophis, Bennu (sampled by OSIRIS-REx), Ryugu (Hayabusa2), Eros (NEAR Shoemaker), Itokawa (Hayabusa)
  and Didymos (DART).
- **Main belt:** Ceres (a dwarf planet, 940 km), Vesta (525 km) and Psyche, a metal world.
- **Kuiper belt (30–55 AU):** Eris, Makemake, Haumea, Quaoar, Gonggong, Arrokoth (visited by New Horizons), Albion
  and a dozen more icy bodies, beyond Neptune and around Pluto. **Sedna** swings out toward the inner Oort cloud.

Icy **I-type** bodies are 55 % water ice: the best fuel stop in the outer Solar System.

## Dangers out there

In the **Kuiper belt** and the **Oort cloud** (2,000–100,000 AU) icy debris hits far more often than near Earth, and
every hit gouges the hull. A Whipple shield stops 90 % of it ([[Parts]]). Proxima Centauri, the nearest star, lies
4.25 light-years away (268,000 AU), beyond the Oort cloud ([[Solar System]], [[Relativity]]).

## What they are made of

| Type | Water ice | Carbon | Silicates | Iron | Nickel | Platinum metals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C** (carbon-rich) | 15 % | 5 % | 70 % | 8 % | 1.5 % | trace |
| **S** (stony) | 1 % | 0.5 % | 75 % | 18 % | 4 % | trace |
| **M** (metallic) | – | 0.2 % | 10 % | 80 % | 9 % | 0.01 % |

C-types are where you get water (for drinking, oxygen and hydrogen fuel); M-types are where you get metal.

## Three ways to mine

| Method | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ⛏ Mining laser (key 1, hold) | vaporizes rock; about 2 MJ per kg | collects 80 % of what it cuts, only within 10 km |
| Weapons | impacts shatter rock; about 0.5 MJ per kg | only 25 % is collected |
| [[Mining drones|Drones and Probes]] | dig 18 kg per hour each while you do other things | stay within 30 km |

Everything you mine is counted by element in the ledger: matter is never created (Law 3,
[[The Laws of Cosmic Impulse]]). Mining contracts pay extra ([[Contracts]]).
