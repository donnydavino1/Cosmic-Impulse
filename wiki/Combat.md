# Combat

| Key | Weapon | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⛏ Mining laser | hold; cuts asteroids into ore, weak against ships |
| 2 | ✦ Pulse laser | 5 MJ per shot at full power; recharge depends on your power bus |
| 3 | ☄ Particle beam | hold; research needed |
| 4 | ⇶ Railgun | 2 kg iron slugs at 8 km/s; aim for the *lead* marker |
| 5 | 🚀 Missiles | homing; start with 4 |

**Range matters.** A laser's spot spreads with distance, so delivered energy falls with the square of range: full
power for the pulse laser inside 50 km, a quarter at 100 km.

**Raider classes:** from wave 2 on, waves mix in other ships. *Interceptors* are fast and fragile and close to 2–4 km;
*gunships* (wave 3+) are slow and tough and fire kinetic slugs from 12–18 km that don't weaken with distance and can
puncture your tanks; *missile boats* (wave 4+) hang back 60–80 km and launch missiles. A message lists each wave's mix,
and labels say which kind each raider is. Each class fights differently: drones circle you, interceptors make fast attack runs past you and swing back,
gunships hold their range and withdraw when badly hurt, and missile boats back away if you close in. Each class has its own ship model, and with sensors at level 3 or better
your radar draws them differently: interceptors as triangles, gunships as squares, missile boats as dotted diamonds.

**Raider waves:** press **0**. Raider drones close in from 25–45 km, circle you and fire lasers; from wave 2 they
fire missiles. Shoot missiles down with the pulse laser (**O** to target them). Beat a wave for missiles and
metals; each wave is bigger. If they win, you lose your ship. Get more than 400 km away and they give up.
Your hull repairs itself between fights using spare-parts kits.

**Where hits land:** a hit strikes the outside of your ship, so big exposed modules (radiator panels, the crew cabin)
are hit most often. A hit on a module damages it (and a badly damaged part fails until repaired), a kinetic hit on the
tanks spills propellant, and a hit on the crew cabin hurts the crew. **Ceramic-composite armour panels** absorb 60 % of
a hit that strikes them; mounted beside a module in the [[Ship Builder]] each one covers 20 % of it, so a ring of armour
around the crew cabin protects them. The builder shows what share of hits each module takes.

**Hiding:** raiders find you by what you give off. Active radar, a running engine or a shot in the last minute
reveals you at any range; a **quiet ship** (passive radar, engine off, no shots) is only seen within 25 km. When they
lose you, they fly to where they last saw you and circle there, and you get a message. See [[Sensors and Radar]].

**Against other players:** see [[Multiplayer]]. Hits only count when both games run the same rules and physics
([[Fair Play and Rules]]).
