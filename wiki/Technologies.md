# Technologies

*Generated from the game's own rules files, so the numbers are always the ones the game uses.*

Research points (RP) come from your crew and labs over time. Each technology may need others first.

## Energy

| Technology | Cost | Needs | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-junction solar cells** | 50 RP | — | Three stacked semiconductor layers catch more of the spectrum: all panels 30% → 40% efficient. |
| **Concentrator solar arrays** | 180 RP | Multi-junction solar cells | Lenses focus sunlight on small high-grade cells: 47% efficient. |
| **Supercapacitors** | 40 RP | — | Charge stored on vast carbon surfaces: little energy, enormous power. |
| **Solid-state batteries** | 70 RP | — | Solid electrolytes: twice the energy per kilogram of Li-ion. |
| **Composite flywheels** | 60 RP | Carbon composites | Carbon rotors spinning in vacuum: low energy density, huge power, no wear. |
| **Space fission reactors** | 100 RP | — | Steady power anywhere, even in shadow or far from the Sun. |
| **Brayton-cycle reactors** | 300 RP | Space fission reactors, High-temperature heat pipes | Megawatt reactors with gas turbines. |
| **Magnetic-confinement fusion** | 1200 RP | Brayton-cycle reactors, High-temperature superconductors | Steady fusion reactors and the magnetic fusion drive: heavy and dependable. |
| **Inertial-confinement fusion** | 1200 RP | Brayton-cycle reactors, Pulsed lasers | Pellet fusion: lighter reactors and the high-thrust pulse drive, but harder on hardware. |
