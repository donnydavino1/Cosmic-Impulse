# Vision and Philosophy

> **Build the spaceship you've always wanted.** Design its engines, weapons, armour, power, interiors and almost
> anything else you can imagine. Then take it into a massive, to-scale solar system to explore, mine, build, and
> battle other players in their own ships.

This page is the promise Cosmic Impulse is built on. Every feature is measured against it.

## 1. Make it yours

Total freedom over how your ship **looks, works and feels**: its shape, layout, modules, graphics, cockpit,
displays, controls, interiors, HUD and sounds. Bring your own art, models, code and designs.

Your customizations never change the underlying rules. Everyone has the same physics, the same mass, energy,
weapon output and sensor limits; you grow those with energy, resources and technology. *How* you read and use
that information is yours: build your own radar screens, targeting aids, automation, warnings and tools. Fly by
hand, automate everything, or invent something nobody has thought of.

**In the game today:** [[Ship Builder]] (module layout that changes performance) · [[Customization]] (style,
hardpoints, cockpit and dashboards) · [[Sensors and Radar]] (radar designs) · [[Build Your Own Cosmic Impulse]]
(rebuild the whole client with an AI).

## 2. Same rules for everyone

Multiplayer runs on one shared set of rules:

- a **to-scale solar system** ([[Solar System]]);
- **Newtonian physics and orbital dynamics** at normal speeds, with **special relativity** and time dilation as you
  approach the speed of light ([[Orbital Mechanics]], [[Relativity]]), using practical approximations where needed;
- **fixed solar output**: the Sun gives a constant amount of energy, and you can harness more the closer you go
  ([[Energy and Power]]);
- **time runs faster than real life** so that collecting energy, processing resources and travelling all happen at
  a playable pace (the vision sets 300× for shared servers; single player lets you choose);
- **resource extraction** from asteroids and other bodies ([[Asteroids and Mining]]);
- **defined scaling** for engines, weapons, armour, power, storage and every other component, with more freedom as
  technology advances ([[Engines]], [[Parts]], [[Technologies]]).

These rules are identical for everyone; a fingerprint proves it ([[Fair Play and Rules]]). The short version of
all of them is [[The Laws of Cosmic Impulse]].

## 3. Almost unlimited creation

The goal goes beyond unlocking recipes in a fixed tree. Two paths are being explored:

- **Player-created systems:** tools to design your own components, recipes, technologies and manufacturing.
- **A massive modular library:** a huge set of components with real material and elemental compositions that you
  combine, modify and experiment with.

**In the game today:** every part has a real mass and recipe ([[Parts]]), modules are arranged freely
([[Ship Builder]]), and drones and probes extend what one ship can do ([[Drones and Probes]]).

## Technology eras

Start in a **Solar Punk** era and move through **Ion → Nuclear → Antimatter → Beyond**. Technology should be
discovered through experimentation, research, resource gathering and invention, not just a fixed ladder.
See [[Technologies]] and [[Engines]].

## Philosophy

### Free

100 % free to play. Real money can never buy ships, weapons, resources, upgrades or any competitive advantage.

### Open source

The long-term goal is a fully open-source game under the **GNU GPLv2**, so players can inspect, modify, improve and
host their own versions. The goal is not just a big list of things to build, but the tools to invent what comes
next. See [[Build Your Own Cosmic Impulse]].

### Real, not cartoon

Space is black and silent, light comes from one star, and ships are machines with mass, heat and limits. The
default look aims for that: physically based lighting and materials, no glowing outlines. (Prefer the old stylized
look? 🎨 Customize → STYLE → *Realistic look*.)
