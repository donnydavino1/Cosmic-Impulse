# Customization

**🎨 Customize & settings (K)** — everything here is looks only; it never changes physics.

## Which release am I playing?

The top of the window, **ABOUT THIS RELEASE**, shows the version (for example **Cosmic Impulse v0.4.0**, “Cinematic”),
its release date, whether this is the computer or the phone build, and two fingerprints: the *code fingerprint* (this
exact file) and the *physics fingerprint* (the rules). Two players can battle fairly when their physics fingerprints
match, even if their versions or looks differ (see [[Fair Play and Rules]]). The welcome screen shows the version too.

## Graphics and interface

- **Look:** *Cinematic* (default from v0.4.0): a deep nebula sky in violet, blue and red, bright foreground stars,
  animated star surfaces (granulation on the Sun, a cracked molten crust on red dwarfs like Proxima Centauri), a
  coloured fill light from the nebula on your ship, lit windows, running lights and finer city lights on Earth's night
  side, atmospheres that glow at the planet's edge and turn orange at sunset, swirling cloud bands on the giant planets,
  exhaust plumes with a white-hot core, and dark rubble-pile asteroids. *Realistic*: real lighting and a black sky with a faint Milky Way. *Stylized*: the original bright colours.
- **Lens effects** (cinematic look): *High* adds bloom, light rays from the Sun that planets and your hull shadow,
  lens flares and horizontal streaks; *Low* keeps only the bloom (phones start here); *Off* draws the plain image.
  Film grain and light rays can be switched separately.
- **Interface:** *Bridge* (default) is a starship-console skin: dark glass panels, condensed labels, one accent colour
  per job (amber for ship and engineering, ice blue for navigation, violet for crew, red for weapons). *Classic HUD*
  brings back the earlier look. Every button stays in the same place.

## Ship and cockpit

- **Language:** English, Español, 中文 (also on the welcome screen, and **F8** cycles). The wiki has the same switch.

- **Style presets:** Sunchaser, Blue Lancer, Corsair, Solar Monk, Ice Hauler, Nebula Runner.
- **Colours, hull shape, wings, solar panel style (holographic or classic).**
- **Trim:** rings, stripes or chevrons. You earn one more trim mark per 7 technologies.
- **Glow strength** and **sensor antenna** (its shape follows your sensor tier).
- **Hardpoints:** move, rotate and resize your antenna and each weapon turret on the hull.
- **Cockpit:** Neo glass theme or classic panels; dashboard widgets you can move, resize, add and remove,
  with several saved layouts (**I** edits, **J** switches).
- **Share your style:** *Copy style as JSON* gives a small text you can send; *Load a style* applies one.

Upgrades show on your ship automatically: better sensors change the antenna, research adds trim, weapons add
turrets that pop out when armed.
