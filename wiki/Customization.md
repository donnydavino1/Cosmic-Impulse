# Customization

**🎨 Customize (K)** — everything here is looks only; it never changes physics.

- **Language:** English, Español, 中文 (also on the welcome screen, and **F8** cycles). The wiki has the same switch.

- **Style presets:** Sunchaser, Blue Lancer, Corsair, Solar Monk, Ice Hauler, Nebula Runner.
- **Colours, hull shape, wings, solar panel style (holographic or classic).**
- **Trim:** rings, stripes or chevrons. You earn one more trim mark per 7 technologies.
- **Glow strength** and **sensor antenna** (its shape follows your sensor tier).
- **Hardpoints:** move, rotate and resize your antenna and each weapon turret on the hull.
- **Cockpit:** Neo glass theme or classic panels; dashboard widgets you can move, resize, add and remove,
  with several saved layouts (**I** edits, **J** switches).
- **Share your style:** *Copy style as JSON* gives a small text you can send; *Load a style* applies one.

Upgrades show on your ship automatically: better sensors change the antenna, research adds trim, weapons add
turrets that pop out when armed.
