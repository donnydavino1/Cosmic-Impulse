# Upgrades

Every part you install can be refined from **Mk I** to **Mk V**. Open the [[Ship Builder]] (key **7**), select a
module, and press **⬆ Upgrade to Mk …**. The upgrade is a job for your fabricator, like building a part
([[Ship and Engineering]]).

## What each mark improves

| Part type | Each mark above Mk I |
|---|---|
| Frames | +15 % g rating (harder thrust before the ship bends) |
| Batteries, flywheels, supercapacitors | +20 % storage and power |
| Generators and reactors | +15 % power, −15 % radiation |
| Radiators | +15 % radiator area, +3 % maximum temperature |
| Life support | −10 % power use |
| Shielding | +15 % shielding, −10 % power use |
| Laboratories | +25 % research speed |
| Fabricators | +25 % manufacturing power |
| Sensor parts | +1 sensor level at Mk III and Mk V, −10 % power use |

A Mk V lab researches twice as fast as a Mk I; a Mk V reactor gives 60 % more power and 60 % less radiation.

## What it costs

- **Materials:** half of the part's own recipe, times its current mark. Mk I → II costs half the recipe, Mk IV → V
  costs twice the recipe.
- **Energy:** doubles with each mark.
- **Knowledge:** Mk III needs 8 technologies known, Mk IV needs 12, Mk V needs 16 ([[Technologies]]).

## Why upgraded parts get heavier

The materials an upgrade uses are built into the part, so the part's mass grows by exactly that much. Nothing
appears from nowhere ([[The Laws of Cosmic Impulse]], Law 3). Heavier parts change your centre of mass and turn
time in the [[Ship Builder]], so upgrades are a trade-off, not a free win.

## Engines

Engines have their own refinement: research **Engine refinement Mk II** and **Mk III**, then upgrade each engine in
🛠 → 🚀 Engines (+12 % exhaust speed, −30 % waste heat, −25 % mass per watt each time). See [[Engines]].
