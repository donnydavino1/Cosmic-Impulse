# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Delta-v | how much your ship can change its speed with the fuel on board |
| Exhaust speed (vₑ) | how fast an engine throws propellant out; higher means more delta-v per kilogram |
| Periapsis / apoapsis | lowest / highest point of an orbit |
| Prograde / retrograde | along / against your direction of motion |
| Proper time | time on your ship's clock; runs slow near light speed |
| γ (gamma) | the relativity factor; 1 at low speed, 7.1 at 0.99c |
| Sv (sievert) | radiation dose; 1 Sv makes you sick, 6 Sv is fatal |
| Ledger | the bookkeeping of everything conserved: mass by element, energy, momentum, time |
| ORB-TLM | the standard format for sensor contacts that every radar display reads |
| Physics fingerprint | a hash of the rules code; equal fingerprints = fair battles |
| Hardpoint | a spot on the hull where a part sits and can be moved |
| Raider | hostile unmanned drone that attacks in waves |
