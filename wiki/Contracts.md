# Contracts

Press **9** (phone: ☰ → Ship → Contracts board) for missions from Earth's mission control. Three are on offer at
a time, picked to suit where you are; you can run up to three at once. New offers appear every game day.

| Contract | Done when |
|---|---|
| Raise your orbit | your lowest point is above the target height over Earth |
| Escape Earth | your path no longer loops back (eccentricity ≥ 1) |
| Reach orbit around a world | that world is your dominant body and you're bound to it |
| Survey an asteroid | you're within 10 km of it |
| Mine rock | you've mined that much since accepting |
| Bounty | you've destroyed that many raiders since accepting |
| Deliver water | you're in orbit around the target with the water aboard (it's used up) |
| Sun dive | you're within the target distance of the Sun |
| Send a science probe | data from a probe you launched to that world reaches you |
| Haul ore with drones | your mining drones have brought back that much since accepting |
| Light-speed trial | you reach 1 % of c (needs more than 15 technologies) |

## How you get paid (and why)

Rewards follow [[The Laws of Cosmic Impulse]], so nothing appears from nowhere:

- **Research points** are knowledge, sent by radio. Instant.
- **Energy** arrives by **power beam** from Earth: only what your battery has room for. A full battery wastes it,
  so make room first.
- **Matter** (materials, spare parts, missiles) comes in a **supply capsule** launched from Earth. It can only
  reach you inside Earth's gravity zone; if you're far away it waits, and docks the next time you're home.

Tip: chain contracts that point the same way. *Escape Earth* + *Reach orbit around Mars* + *Deliver water to Mars*
is one trip.
