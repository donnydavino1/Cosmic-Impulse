# Propulsion Concepts

Every engine in Cosmic Impulse is a real design: some have flown for decades, some have been tested on the ground, and
some are serious proposals from engineers and physicists. All of them follow the same rules: thrust = k × power ÷
exhaust speed, and the rocket equation ([[Orbital Mechanics]]). Numbers for each engine are on [[Engines]].

| Engine | Status in the real world | Why you would pick it |
|---|---|---|
| Chemical rocket | flown since the 1940s | huge thrust; escapes planets quickly |
| Ion thruster (gridded) | flown on many deep-space probes | very fuel-efficient on xenon |
| Hall-effect thruster | flown on hundreds of satellites | more thrust per watt than gridded ion, cheaper |
| Arcjet | flown on communication satellites | simple electric thrust from hydrogen |
| Solar thermal rocket | tested on the ground | twice chemical exhaust speed with no reactor, best near the Sun |
| Helicon magnetoplasma engine | tested in vacuum chambers | high power electric drive on hydrogen you make from water |
| MPD thruster | tested in laboratories | megawatt-class electric thrust |
| Nuclear thermal rocket | ground-tested reactors in the 1960s–70s, new programmes today | strong thrust at twice chemical exhaust speed |
| Solar sail | flown on small demonstration spacecraft | no fuel at all near the Sun |
| Electric sail | proposed; tether experiments in orbit | no fuel, and it fades only as 1/distance, so it works far out |
| Gas-core nuclear rocket | proposed | 30 km/s with enormous thrust |
| Direct fusion drive | proposed; small experiments running | a fusion engine small enough for a spacecraft |
| Magnetic-confinement fusion drive | proposed | 5 % of light speed exhaust |
| Inertial-fusion pulse drive | proposed (studies of interstellar probes) | lighter and stronger than steady fusion |
| Beamed-core antimatter drive | proposed | a third of light speed |
| Antimatter photon rocket | theoretical | the only way to 99 % of light speed |

## Choosing an engine

- **Leaving a planet** needs thrust: chemical, nuclear thermal, gas-core.
- **Long trips between planets** need exhaust speed: ion, Hall, magnetoplasma, direct fusion.
- **Free rides**: solar sails near the Sun, electric sails in the outer Solar System.
- **The stars** need fusion or antimatter ([[Relativity]]).

You can own several engines and switch between them; unused ones still add mass at the tail ([[Ship Builder]]).
