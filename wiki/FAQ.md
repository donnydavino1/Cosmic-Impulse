# FAQ

**Is it realistic?** Orbits, rocket equation, sunlight, heat, radiation and relativity use real physics and units.
Some things are simplified (and a few are game choices, like raiders). Cinematic space debris is decoration you
can turn off.

**Does it work on my phone?** Yes: `stellar-impulse-mobile.html` is the same game with touch controls and a ☰ menu
containing every action.

**My friend and I can't hurt each other.** Your physics fingerprints differ: use the same version, or the official
rules. See [[Fair Play and Rules]].

**I lost my ship.** You restart in low Earth orbit; research is kept. Save often (🛠 → 💾 Save).

**How do I get everything to test?** **F9**, or 🛠 → 🧪 Testing.

**Can my AI make me a totally different cockpit?** Yes, and you can still battle others. See
[[Build Your Own Stellar Impulse]].
