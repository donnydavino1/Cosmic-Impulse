# Your First 10 Hours

A route through the game, step by step. Every step links to the page that explains it.

## Hour 1: learn to fly

1. **Look around.** Press **V** to cycle cameras: behind the ship, cockpit, orbit map ([[Controls]]).
2. **Raise your orbit.** In orbit mode, hold **W** a few seconds and watch the orange loop grow on the map
   ([[Flying and Orbits]]).
3. **Pick a cockpit style and radar** in 🎨 Customize (**K**): try the rotating *sonar* radar ([[Cockpit and Displays]]).

## Hours 2–3: power and research

4. **Collect energy.** Your solar panels fill the battery; buy more panels in 🛠 Engineering (**B**)
   ([[Energy and Power]]).
5. **Research.** Your lab earns research points; spend them in 🛠 → 🔬 Research. *Onboard laboratory* first: it
   makes all research 6× faster ([[Technologies]]).
6. **Take contracts** (**9**): raise your orbit, survey an asteroid. They pay in research, energy and supplies
   ([[Contracts]]).

## Hours 3–5: leave Earth and mine

7. **Escape Earth.** Your chemical rocket carries enough fuel ([[Orbital Mechanics]]).
8. **Fly to an asteroid** with 🧭 NAVIGATE (**N**) → Asteroids; the autopilot parks 1.5 km away.
9. **Mine it** with the mining laser (hold **1**), or research robotics and send drones (**8**)
   ([[Asteroids and Mining]], [[Drones and Probes]]).

## Hours 5–8: build a real ship

10. **Fabricate parts** from what you mined: radiators, better batteries, a reactor ([[Parts]], [[Heat and Radiators]]).
11. **Arrange your modules** in the 🧱 Ship Builder (**7**): crew at the front, tanks next to them, reactor far
    behind on a truss ([[Ship Builder]], [[Radiation]]).
12. **Upgrade** your lab and fabricator first: faster research and building pay off everywhere ([[Upgrades]]).

## Hours 8–10: go further

13. **Send a science probe** to Mars or Jupiter; launch from low orbit to save xenon ([[Drones and Probes]]).
14. **Fight raiders** (**0**): build the pulse laser first; lasers hit hardest inside 50 km ([[Combat]]).
15. **Play with a friend** (**P**) ([[Multiplayer]]), or aim for the stars: fusion and antimatter drives lead
    toward the speed of light ([[Relativity]]).

Stuck? Press **C** for the in-game guide, or read the [[FAQ]].
