Speed from fuel

Δv = vₑ · ln(m₀ / m₁)

vₑ is the exhaust speed, m₀ the mass with fuel, m₁ without. Doubling the fuel does not double the speed.

Near light speed

v = c · tanh((vₑ/c) · ln(m₀/m₁))

The same law in special relativity. It never reaches c; the game caps ships at 0.99 c.

Thrust from power

F = k · P / vₑ

For the same power, a faster exhaust pushes more gently. That is why efficient engines are slow to accelerate.

Burn time

t = m_fuel · vₑ² / (k · P)

Fuel flows at k·P/vₑ². A bigger engine (more power P) burns the same fuel faster; ship mass only matters through how much fuel you need.

Ideal numbers: they assume full thrust the whole time. In the game, overheating (not enough radiators), the crew's g-limit, or not enough electric power for electric engines can hold the throttle down and make burns longer. Engine size is the game's engine resize (power × mass of the engine). The dashed lines mark speed changes from low Earth orbit: 3.2 km/s escapes Earth, about 9 km/s reaches 0.2 AU from the Sun, about 21 km/s falls into the Sun.
